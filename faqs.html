Getting Started

3 questions
TechPath is an integrated engineering education operating system designed to guide undergraduate students from their first semester to their dream career. It unites semester-specific video lectures (LearnHub), AI document notes synthesis, doubt resolution, in-browser isolated code execution, resume building, and AI mock interviews into one unified dashboard.
Click Start Your Journey from the homepage. Select your engineering department (e.g. Computer Science, Electronics, Mechanical, Civil) and your current semester (1st through 8th). The platform will automatically personalize your study roadmaps, recommended YouTube lectures, and syllabus exam trackers.
Public pages—including this FAQ, Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, Reviews, and Contact Us—are open to everyone without login. However, accessing personalized workspaces, saving AI Notes, tracking daily study time, and participating in mock interviews requires creating a free student account.

AI Notes & Study Packs

7 questions
You can upload lecture slide decks, syllabus outlines, textbook chapter excerpts, lab manuals, and handwritten notes in PDF, TXT, or image formats.
Yes. AI Notes supports multi-document uploads. The system extracts and stitches together relevant text across your uploaded files to construct unified topic study packs.
Yes. When an uploaded PDF contains little or no extractable digital text (e.g. scanned textbook pages or phone snapshots), TechPath triggers client-side OCR (Optical Character Recognition) fallback to transcribe text before note generation.
If digital extraction yields under threshold word counts, the system notifies you and initiates an OCR pass on the document pages. If the document is illegible or corrupted, the system alerts you so you can upload a clearer file.
Generated notes follow a structured technical format: Executive Overview, Key Definitions, Core Formulas & Equations, Step-by-Step Theorems/Algorithms, Real-World Engineering Applications, and High-Yield Exam Review Tips.
Yes. You can merge notes from multiple lecture units or chapters into a comprehensive Master Study Pack for mid-semester or final exam revision.
Yes. Each study pack generated by AI Notes includes interactive flashcards with active recall prompts and practice quiz questions to test your conceptual understanding before exams.
