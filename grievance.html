Grievance Redressal Mechanism

Statutory Mandate: Rule 3(2), IT Rules 2021 & Sec. 13, DPDP Act 2023 • Response SLA: 24–48 Hours Acknowledgement • Resolution SLA: Within 15 Days

In compliance with the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021, and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023, TechPath provides a dedicated, accessible, and accountable mechanism to address user grievances, privacy rights, and content disputes.

2. Scope of Grievances Handled

The Grievance Redressal Officer investigates complaints falling into the following statutory categories:

1. Data Privacy & Correction Requests for access, correction, erasure, or extraction of your student profile and learning metrics.
2. Consent Withdrawal Formal withdrawal of consent for processing academic profile or analytical data under the DPDP Act.
3. Copyright & Takedowns Notice of infringement regarding unauthorized course notes, slide decks, or proprietary materials.
4. Review Moderation & Abuse Complaints regarding abusive, defamatory, harassing, or fraudulent peer reviews or community posts.
5. Account Access & Security Compromised accounts, session hijacking reports, or technical vulnerabilities discovered.
6. Minor / Child Safety Inquiries or deletion requests from parents or guardians concerning users under 18 years of age.
03

3. DPDP Act Right to Withdraw Consent & Erasure

Under Section 6(4) of the DPDP Act, 2023, data principals have the right to withdraw consent at any time with the same ease with which it was granted.

How to Withdraw Consent or Purge Your Account:

To revoke consent or request permanent erasure of your account, email lakkimsettirahulashokh@gmail.com with the subject line "DPDP Consent Revocation / Account Erasure" from your registered email address.

Upon receipt, our backend administrators will:

  • Deactivate active sessions and revoke authentication tokens immediately.
  • Purge personal profile rows, uploaded avatar images, and study notes from PostgreSQL database storage within thirty (30) days.
  • Notify third-party subprocessors to cease processing any data derived from your account.
04

4. Step-by-Step Filing Procedure

To enable swift investigation, please ensure your complaint email contains:

  1. Full Name & Registered Email: The exact email tied to your TechPath account.
  2. Clear Description: A concise explanation of the incident, concern, or right being invoked.
  3. Supporting URL / Evidence: Exact link(s) to the disputed content, error logs, or screenshots.
  4. Remedy Requested: Explicitly state the outcome sought (e.g., removal of content, account deletion, security remediation).
05

5. Mandatory Timelines & Service Level Agreements (SLAs)

We adhere to strict statutory response deadlines:

Within 24 to 48 Hours Formal Acknowledgement & Ticket ID

Your grievance is acknowledged with a unique tracking identifier and initial triage classification.

Within 15 Days Investigation & Final Redressal

Comprehensive resolution, content removal, or remedial action completed and communicated in writing.

06

6. Escalation Matrix & Appellate Remedies

If you are not satisfied with the resolution provided by our Grievance Officer, you hold the statutory right to appeal:

  • Data Protection Board of India (DPBI): Under the DPDP Act, 2023, data principals may register appeals with the Data Protection Board once operationalized by the Central Government.
  • Grievance Appellate Committee (GAC): Users aggrieved by orders of the Grievance Officer regarding content removal may appeal before the Grievance Appellate Committee established by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY).
