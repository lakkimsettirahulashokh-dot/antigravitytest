2. Indian Copyright Act & Fair Dealing Provisions

TechPath is governed under the laws of India. Under Section 52 of the Copyright Act, 1957, certain acts of "fair dealing" with literary, musical, or artistic works are explicitly permitted and do not constitute an infringement of copyright:

  • Private or Personal Use & Research: Section 52(1)(a)(i) recognizes fair dealing for private study, non-commercial educational research, and individual scholarship.
  • Instruction in Educational Institutions: Section 52(1)(i) permits reproduction of work in the course of instruction or as part of examination questions.

TechPath's AI features are structured solely to assist individual students in their private study and research under these fair dealing principles. Users may not use TechPath to mass-extract or facilitate pirate distribution of copyrighted publications.

3. Scope of Processing License Granted to TechPath

By uploading documents to TechPath, you grant TechPath a strictly limited, non-exclusive, royalty-free license solely to:

  • Parse document layout and extract text strings locally or via secure transient server memory.
  • Execute optical character recognition (OCR) on image-based lecture slides to make them readable for the student.
  • Send extracted textual prompts through server-side AI language model proxies to fulfill your explicit query (e.g., generating 5 revision flashcards or answering a specific doubt).

TechPath does not use your private uploaded documents to train public foundation models, nor are your private files visible to or indexed for other students.

5. Ephemeral Retention & Deletion Lifecycle

Uploaded files in the PDF Analyzer and AI Notes are treated with strict ephemeral lifecycle policies:

Local Browser Processing Most PDF text extraction executes directly in your browser memory via WebAssembly/PDF.js without persistent disk caching.
Temporary Server Buffers Where OCR or server processing is invoked, file buffers are discarded immediately upon completion of generation.

6. Notice and Takedown Procedure (DMCA / Indian IT Rules)

TechPath operates as an intermediary under Section 79 of the Information Technology Act, 2000, and complies with international DMCA notice standards. If you are a copyright owner or authorized agent and believe that content accessible on TechPath infringes your copyright, please submit a written notification containing:

  1. Identification of the copyrighted work claimed to have been infringed, or a representative list of works.
  2. Identification of the material claimed to be infringing and its specific URL or location on TechPath.
  3. Your contact information, including physical address, telephone number, and official email address.
  4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  5. A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notification is accurate and that you are authorized to act on behalf of the owner.
  6. A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or authorized representative.
