1. Introduction

Welcome to TechPath ("TechPath", "the platform", "we", "us", or "our"). TechPath is an educational technology operating system developed to empower engineering students through personalized learning curricula, AI-assisted study notes, interactive coding environments, doubt solving, and career readiness tools.

We believe trust is fundamental to education. This Privacy Policy details the exact types of information gathered when you access our website, use our student workspace, interact with our AI agents, or upload educational files, and outlines how that information is safeguarded.

02

2. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to operate our academic tools and personalize your study journey. This includes:

  • User-Provided Data: Data you explicitly submit when creating an account, personalizing your profile, uploading study notes or resumes, posting reviews, or submitting support queries.
  • Operational & Activity Data: Active study duration, LearnHub video engagement, coding IDE executions, and progress on exam trackers.
  • Technical Diagnostic Data: Basic browser metadata, network timing, and client runtime error diagnostics required for server stability and security.
03

3. Information You Provide

When engaging with the various features of TechPath, you may voluntarily submit:

Registration Details Email address, display name, and securely salted credentials.
Academic Preferences Engineering department, semester level, target skills, and target companies.
Documents & Media Textbook excerpts, lecture slide PDFs, and resume documents.
Communication Submissions Contact form inquiries, bug reports, and peer reviews.
04

4. Account and Authentication Information

TechPath uses Supabase Authentication as our identity infrastructure:

  • Email and Password Authentication: Passwords are cryptographically salted and hashed on Supabase's secure auth tier. TechPath staff never view, store, or have access to raw plaintext passwords.
  • Google Authentication: When you select "Continue with Google", authentication is performed through Google OAuth 2.0. We receive only your verified email, display name, and unique user ID from Google. We do not obtain access to your Google Drive, Gmail, or external files.
  • Password Reset: Password reset requests are managed via cryptographically signed time-limited recovery tokens dispatched to your registered email address.
05

5. Profile Information

Your profile allows TechPath to tailor educational recommendations:

  • Department & Semester: Used to filter curriculum modules, recommended YouTube lectures, and syllabus study packs.
  • Profile Picture: You may voluntarily upload a custom profile avatar. Avatars are stored in dedicated Supabase storage buckets or stored locally in your browser session. You can change or remove your profile picture at any time.
  • Bio & Skills: Saved locally or to your authenticated profile to assist the Resume Builder in organizing technical experience.
06

6. Uploaded Files and Documents

When using AI Notes, Resume Builder, or Mock Interview, you may upload documents including:

  • Lecture slides and syllabus documents (PDFs, text files).
  • Student resumes and CVs (PDF, DOCX).
  • Assignment problem statements or diagram images (PNG, JPG, WEBP).

Processing Lifecycle: Uploaded files are parsed client-side using PDF.js and optical character recognition (OCR) where applicable. Document text is extracted specifically to fulfill your request (generating structured notes, flashcards, or practice interview questions). Uploaded files are not permanently broadcasted or shared with other students.

07

7. AI Features and Processing

TechPath incorporates AI-assisted capabilities through secure server-side proxies:

  • AI Notes & Study Pack Generator: Analyzes extracted document text to create summaries, key concepts, formulas, and flashcards.
  • AI Doubt Solver: Processes student programming queries, engineering mathematics problems, or code snippets to generate step-by-step pedagogical explanations.
  • AI Mock Interview: Synthesizes role-specific technical and behavioral questions based on your resume or selected engineering domain. When you record practice answers, audio/video data is processed locally or via temporary session buffers. Camera and microphone access is requested strictly when you start a recording session and is never monitored in the background.
info Verification Notice: AI-generated outputs may occasionally contain factual errors or hallucinations. Students should always cross-verify critical academic formulas, university regulations, and career details with authoritative textbooks and official university syllabi.
08

8. Learning Activity and Study Time

To calculate your "Today's Study" metric accurately on the Dashboard, TechPath measures active learning engagement:

  • Active watch time on curated LearnHub video lectures.
  • Active interaction within the Coding IDE sandbox (keystrokes, code execution runs).
  • Active reading and generation in AI Notes and AI Doubt Solver.

We explicitly discard passive idle time when a tab is left open in the background without student activity. Study durations are stored locally and periodically synchronized with your authenticated profile.

Student Reviews: Reviews submitted through the Reviews page are reviewed by platform administrators before publication to prevent spam and maintain high-quality engineering feedback. Published reviews display your designated display name, department, and rating.

Contact Messages: Inquiries sent via the Contact Us form are dispatched securely to our support desk (lakkimsettirahulashokh@gmail.com) to investigate technical issues, academic feedback, or account assistance.

10. How We Use Information

Information collected is utilized exclusively for educational and platform purposes:

  • Personalizing semester roadmaps, recommended YouTube engineering videos, and exam preparation trackers.
  • Executing code snippets in an isolated sandbox and returning syntax/runtime diagnostics.
  • Facilitating secure login sessions and enforcing role-based permissions.
  • Providing customer support and debugging reported platform errors.
  • Defending against distributed attacks, credential abuse, and unauthorized automated scraping.
11

11. How Information Is Stored

User data is stored across two primary persistence layers:

  • Cloud Database & Storage: Hosted on Supabase (PostgreSQL), utilizing encrypted storage volumes and access-restricted storage buckets for user avatar images.
  • Browser Local Storage: Non-sensitive client preferences (dark mode tokens, active study stopwatch snapshots, cached study schedules) are stored directly in your device's browser localStorage for low-latency performance and offline resilience.
12

12. Data Security

We employ rigorous industry best practices to protect your data:

  • Row Level Security (RLS): Database tables enforce PostgreSQL RLS policies, ensuring students can only query and mutate their own profile records, notes, and study metrics.
  • Encrypted Data Transmission: All data exchanged between your browser and our backend server is transmitted over HTTPS utilizing TLS encryption.
  • Zero Browser API Key Leakage: Sensitive third-party AI provider keys and service credentials remain strictly isolated on the backend Node.js server and are never delivered to the client browser.
13

13. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share data only in the following strictly limited situations:

  • Infrastructure Providers: Cloud hosting, database, and authentication services necessary to deliver the TechPath platform (e.g. Supabase).
  • AI Processing Partners: Anonymized or user-submitted prompt text forwarded to AI model APIs solely for the real-time generation of your requested educational output.
  • Legal & Safety Compliance: Where required by valid legal process or to protect the vital security of students and platform infrastructure against malicious cyber threats.

14. Third-Party Services

TechPath integrates only the third-party providers required to fulfill platform functionality:

Supabase (PostgreSQL, Auth & Storage) Provides encrypted database records, user authentication sessions, and avatar storage.
Google Identity Services Enables voluntary Google Single Sign-On (OAuth).

15. Cookies and Local Storage

We use standard browser cookies and local storage mechanisms for necessary operational needs:

  • Session Tokens: Storing cryptographic session credentials to keep you signed in between page navigations.
  • Preferences: Saving dark mode preferences, 3D WebGL motion toggles, and sidebar collapse states.
  • Timer Checkpoints: Preserving active study stopwatch counts so progress is not lost upon accidental browser refreshes.

You can clear browser local storage or disable cookies at any time via your browser settings, though doing so may require signing in on each visit.

17. User Choices and Controls

We provide students with direct control over their information:

  • Motion Preferences: You can disable 3D WebGL background motion via system accessibility settings (prefers-reduced-motion).

18. Data Retention

We retain your profile data, study records, and submitted reviews for as long as your account remains active, or as necessary to provide educational continuity across your engineering semesters. Transient data, such as real-time code executions in the IDE or temporary PDF OCR buffers, is removed promptly following execution or session completion.
